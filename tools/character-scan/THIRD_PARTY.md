# Optional local semantic model

BiSeNet ResNet18 ONNX by Yakhyokhuja Valikhujaev:
https://github.com/yakhyo/face-parsing

The weights are downloaded separately, not embedded in the addon archive.
Pinned SHA-256: `0d9bd318e46987c3bdbfacae9e2c0f461cae1c6ac6ea6d43bbe541a91727e33f`.

claim for Aiko or an independent validation of the training data.
